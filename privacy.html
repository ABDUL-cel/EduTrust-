Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

EduTrust collects academic, administrative, and contact information necessary to manage school records, process student grades, and provide portal access to students, teachers, and parents.

Data stored in EduTrust is exclusively utilized to perform core administrative tasks, process student result sheets, and provide authorized access to administrative user roles.
