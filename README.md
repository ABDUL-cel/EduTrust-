# EduTrust 🎓🤝
**Building Trust Through Transparency**

EduTrust is a school fee management platform that connects schools and parents with clear, simple, and transparent payment records.

No more lost receipts, payment confusion, or lack of trust. EduTrust digitizes everything.

## ✨ Features

- **School Management** - Manage classes, sessions, terms, and students in one place
- **Fee Management** - Create, organize, and track school fees easily
- **Digital Records** - Keep clear records of payments and generate receipts
- **Parent Transparency** - Parents can view what has been paid and what remains
- **Reports & Insights** - Understand your school's payment activities
- **Secure Information** - All school and payment data is organized and protected
