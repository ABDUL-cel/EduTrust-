Frequently Asked Questions

Find fast answers to common questions about setting up and using EduTrust.

How quickly can our school onboard with EduTrust?

Onboarding usually takes under 24 hours. You can import student and teacher profiles via CSV or register accounts individually through your admin dashboard.

Is automated grade calculation supported?

Yes. Once continuous assessment (CA) and examination scores are entered into the gradebook, EduTrust automatically processes totals, grades, positions, and remarks.

Can parents access student result portals on mobile devices?

Yes. The parent dashboard is fully mobile-responsive and accessible on smartphones, tablets, or desktop web browsers.

Is local offline network deployment supported?

Yes, EduTrust can be deployed locally across campus local area networks (LAN) for offline operation without active internet dependency.
